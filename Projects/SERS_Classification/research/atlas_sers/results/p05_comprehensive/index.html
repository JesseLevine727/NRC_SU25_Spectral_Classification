How to read these figures

  • Paired performance plots (scatter dot) show station–instrument domain means over common complete contexts; a point above the equality line means the new CNN is better.
  • Paired delta plots use x = new − reference (positive = better) and y = reference balanced accuracy (BA).
  • Points in the paired plots average common complete contexts within each domain; they are not independent samples and carry no confidence intervals or significance tests.
  • D0-M is the matched control; D0-ERM is the historical CNN; C_SELECTED is the source-selected classical procedure (8 references missing, 252/260 complete; all other references are 260/260 complete).
  • M01 = individual spectra; M06 = combined model probabilities per physical sample (not averaged raw spectra).
  • In source-versus-outer plots each dot is a context/strategy, not a physical sample; the source was used in selection and is a different estimator from the outer ensemble, so the difference is not an unbiased transfer gap.
  • Best-epoch points are the fraction of source fits choosing each best checkpoint, not terminal training epochs or loss curves.
  • Reliability bins are repeated context–spectrum/master appearances, not independent samples; pooled ECE differs from the mean per-context ECE.
  • An empty source-selection panel means support was unavailable, not a failed measurement.
