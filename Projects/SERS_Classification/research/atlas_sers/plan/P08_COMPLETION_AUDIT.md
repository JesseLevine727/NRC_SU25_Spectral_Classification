## 2. Unresolved owner choices

These choices were already submitted; this checkpoint records their dependency rather than asking again or adopting a default.

1. **Normalization selector:** repeat full source-only family selection, or freeze each context's MIN-selected family and retune only its hyperparameters. The four-control ceilings are **525,288** versus **42,368 model fits**, with **1,040 scalar temperatures** in either case. These answer different questions; the smaller option is not an approved substitute.
2. **Regenerated populations:** repeat source-only model selection in the notes-clear and Mira-1-excluded tiers before freezing neural identities, or specify a fixed-model sensitivity. New physical-master roles and support must be enumerated rather than copying the primary contexts.
3. **Perturbation placement:** alter the shared interpolated spectrum before the numerical preprocessing pipeline, or alter the final model input. These test different failure mechanisms.
4. **Synthetic shift edges:** approve or replace the proposed constant endpoint extension for the ±5 cm⁻¹ shift. Unmeasured edge values cannot be presented as observed Raman signal.

Other perturbation details still require an explicit numerical specification: discrete severity levels where not already fixed, impulse magnitudes, random replicates, source-noise estimation, zero-dose parity and statistical contrast families. The current goal does not authorize obtaining these choices from new held-test outcomes.

## 3. Next completion path

First finish the inherited source-kernel/controller integration against the existing input, job, journal and artifact contracts. Test failure, interruption, resource exhaustion and refused recovery using synthetic fixtures. Preserve successful and failed attempts; do not add fits to repair an integration check.

After the required owner choices, complete the affected later-branch numerical specifications, exact operation ledgers and finite resource proposals. Then perform a final requirement-wide audit, publish only reviewed public artifacts, verify the remote revision and CI, and submit the separate bounded scientific execution request. Staged execution remains universal first, adaptive second and robustness/exploratory branches last.

The eventual P08 outcome checklist in Master Plan §27 requires policy effects, routing, preservation and degradation results. Those results belong to later authorized scientific execution; this readiness goal must specify their production and verification without fabricating or prematurely computing them. Minimal min–max remains the reference, not an established optimum.
