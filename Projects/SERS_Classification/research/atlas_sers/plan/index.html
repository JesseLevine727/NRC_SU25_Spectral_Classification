Research direction

LayerQuestionDecision
PrimaryDoes acquisition-aware deep learning beat the source-only development-selected classical pipeline on an unseen instrument and unseen physical masters?Unweighted mean balanced accuracy across 13 domains; paired hierarchical uncertainty.
FallbackAre rigorously tuned classical models the more reliable solution for this small heterogeneous field dataset?Publish the leakage-controlled benchmark and negative deep result if the promotion gate fails.
SecondaryHow do UDA, paired calibration, few-shot labels, preprocessing, quality, and input perturbations change performance?Keep information regimes separate from zero-shot results.
Open setCan a station-conditioned model reject one held nonblank chemical?Narrow eight-task stress test only; no broad open-world claim.
