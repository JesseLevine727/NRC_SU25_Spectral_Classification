The rejected store was never applied or executed.
- **Refit registry (T022): 14 synthetic tests passed.** Source decisions retain the tested selector's complete threshold evidence. A real-metadata dry check with explicitly synthetic constant scores reconciled 320 contexts, 2,880 strategy/seed aliases and 1,920 unique refits under that synthetic all-fallback scenario. These are implementation checks, not actual recipe-selection results.

## Dataset boundary found during review

All 320 outer fitting roles fit within the sampler ceiling; the largest would draw 38 rows, below 48. Test-fold class support is not uniformly three: 279 folds contain three chemicals, 40 contain two and one contains one. A newly added registry check initially rejected these existing sparse test folds. It was corrected to require all three training classes and a nonempty test-class subset, preserving the frozen split population. No fold was removed or changed. Later comparisons must retain the original P03/P04 endpoint metrics and expose absent-class support; a fold without a chemical cannot estimate sensitivity for that chemical.
